Join our team

We're a friendly, close-knit office that's been caring for Miami and surrounding communities for over 40 years. We work hard to preserve and protect our culture, so it stays a place people are glad to come to work.

  • Benefits that matterPaid time off, a retirement plan, and help with your own glasses and contacts, all starting after an introductory period.
  • Training from day oneExperience helps, but it isn't required. You'll learn the equipment, the charts and the rhythm of the day alongside people who've done it for years.
