Website privacy

This page is about this website only. How we protect your health information as a patient is explained in our Notice of Privacy Practices. Ask our front desk for a copy.

What we count

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit this website and how they find us. It tells us things like:

  • which pages were viewed
  • how visitors found the site (for example a Google search, our Google listing or Facebook)
  • the type of device (phone or computer) and a general area, such as the city
  • which buttons were tapped, such as Call, Directions, our email address or Reorder contacts

A tap on Call only tells us the button was tapped. It doesn't tell us whether a call was made or what was said.

What we never collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address or anything you type is never sent to Google Analytics.
  • No health information, appointment details or insurance information.
  • No advertising tracking: Google's advertising features are turned off, and we don't use a Facebook (Meta) pixel.
  • We don't sell or share visitor information.

Cookies

Google Analytics stores a small cookie on your device so it can tell a new visitor from a returning one. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on.

Please don't send medical information online

Please don't email us health information. To talk about your eyes or an appointment, call us at (918) 540-1929. Information sent with a job application is used only for hiring.
